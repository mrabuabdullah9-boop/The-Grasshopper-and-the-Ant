2Key Vocabulary

Look at each picture and listen to the word.

ant

A small insect.

grasshopper

An insect that can jump.

Learn from a book.

clean

Make a place tidy.

fish

Catch fish.

carry food

Take food to a place.

skateboard

Ride on a skateboard.

3Grammar Focus

Use is or are with the -ing form of the verb.

is

Use is with one person or one animal.

The ant is studying.
The grasshopper is playing.
The ant is cleaning.

are

Use are with more than one person or animal.

They are carrying food.
They are eating.
They are working.

-ing

Add -ing to show an action happening now.

study → studying
play → playing
carry → carrying
